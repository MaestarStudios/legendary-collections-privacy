🏆 Legendary Collections

Privacy Policy
Last Updated: May 29, 2026

Overview

Legendary Collections is developed by Maestar Studios, based in Ontario, Canada. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding that information.

What We Collect

Legendary Collections collects only the data you choose to enter: • Your collection data (item names, descriptions, brands, lines, categories, condition, status, and notes) stored entirely on your device. • Photos you take or import from your device's gallery, stored in the app's private storage folder on your device. • UPC/barcode information you voluntarily submit to the community database through the Contribute feature. Submitted entries may include item name, brand, line, category, year, and photos. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, or any other personally identifiable information.

How Your Data Is Stored

All collection data and photos are stored locally on your device using Android's private app storage. This data is not accessible to other apps and is removed when you uninstall Legendary Collections. Backups you create are stored as .lcbackup files in your chosen location. Backups may optionally be stored temporarily on the Legendary Collections community server (operated by Maestar Studios) to assist with device transfers. These backup files contain no personally identifiable information. Temporary backups are automatically deleted after 14 days.

Community UPC Database

If you choose to use the optional community UPC database feature, item information and photos you submit are sent to the Legendary Collections community server operated by Maestar Studios. Submissions are reviewed before being made available to other users. No account or personal information is required to submit or look up entries. Submissions are associated only with the data you provide (barcode, item details, and photos).

Optional Server Connection

Legendary Collections can optionally connect to the Legendary Collections community server for backup, restore, and community UPC features. When connected, your collection backup data and submitted UPC entries are transmitted to that server over your local network or internet connection. Backup files stored on the server contain no personally identifiable information and are automatically deleted after 14 days.

Third-Party Services

Legendary Collections does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to any third party.

Children's Privacy

Legendary Collections does not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13. The app contains no features designed to collect personal information from children.

Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over it at all times. You may: • Delete any item, photo, or collection data directly within the app. • Wipe all app data using the Developer Settings option or Android's app management settings. • Uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data permanently.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The app will notify you of any updates and ask you to review and accept the revised policy. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy reflects the most recent revision.
